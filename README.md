# 🎯 SeatSpot – Scalable Shared Transit MVP for Tier-2/3 Cities

## ⏰ The Problem
Every day, office-goers, jobholders, and students in Tier-2 cities waste 15–20 minutes standing on the road, waiting for a shared auto-rickshaw. Even if they find one, drivers refuse to move until they fill the very last seat. This makes everyone late almost every single day.

## 🚀 The Solution
To solve this daily headache, I designed a simple app concept called **"SeatSpot"** to make our daily travel completely stress-free. Now, users can just open the map from their home and see exactly how many auto-rickshaws are available on the road right now!

### ✨ Key Features:
* **Zero Waiting:** Open the app from home, track live autos on the road, and time your departure perfectly.
* **"Quick Destination" Feature:** One-tap automated booking for regular daily routes (Office, Job, or College)—no typing needed on busy mornings.
* **"1-Seat Left" Alert:** The live map visually highlights near-full autos that need **just ONE more passenger** in Green. Walk straight to it, sit, and leave instantly!
* **Same Daily Fare:** Keeps the exact same local per-seat fare, making it affordable for everyday use.
* **Edge-Case Resolution:** Engineered a digital seat-locking mechanism with a 3-minute secure timer and OTP verification to prevent allocation conflicts among multiple users.

## 📊 Tech, Data & Success Metrics
* **User Adoption:** Targeting **10,000+ Daily Active Users (DAU)** via simple in-app analytics tools.
* **Business Impact:** Includes an Instagram-style activity dashboard for drivers to reduce vehicle idle time, aimed at **increasing daily driver earnings by 20%**.
